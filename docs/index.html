Access isn't a privacy policy. It's the architecture.

An AI reaches your records only through a device you've unlocked. There's no other door — so nothing can query your health in the background while you're asleep or away.

🔓 Device unlocked → Scoped session → AI can query

You explicitly pair the AI; your unlocked device decrypts, answers, and re-seals. Pairings expire, and closing the tab ends access immediately.

🔒 Locked / offline → Request denied

No unlocked device means nothing can decrypt the vault. The request simply can't be answered — there's no key anywhere else to answer it with.

The serverMoves sealed frames between the AI and your device. Never holds a key, never sees plaintext.
Your deviceHolds the only key. Decrypts on demand, only while unlocked and present.
The AI you chooseSees exactly what you send it for that task — and nothing when you're gone.

Trust, proven — not asserted

We hold none of your data, and we test that in the open.

🔐 Zero-knowledge vault

Passkey unlock over a random key. No password to phish, no server-side secret to steal. A breach yields sealed data.

🧪 We attack our own product

Internal, AI-assisted adversarial reviews with frontier models — findings published in full, not buried. Independent third-party audit is on the roadmap. Read the review →

📖 Honest boundaries

We publish what we can and can't see, and where the limits are — on purpose. Trust model →

Straight about the edges: what you choose to send an AI provider is seen by that provider once it arrives — we control whether and what leaves and stay blind to it, but we don't govern their retention. Web-delivered crypto trusts the origin; we mitigate and disclose it. Self-hosting means the trust is yours to hold.
