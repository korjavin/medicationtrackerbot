Private by architecture

Your devices hold the keys. The server holds a sealed blob.

There are two kinds of place your data can live, and this app is careful about which is which.

Your devices

Keys & plaintext

Encryption and decryption happen on your device. A passkey unwraps a random data key that never leaves it — there's no password to steal and no server-side secret to crack.

The server

A blind sealed blob

A blind sync-and-backup hub that stores ciphertext and rings your reminders on schedule. A breach, subpoena, or curious operator yields ciphertext and timing metadata — not your health.

The hard parts survive the encryption — reminders, chat, mobile.

Blind push relay

Reminder content is encrypted before upload. The relay knows when to ring, not what for. Your device decrypts and shows the real text.

On-device notifications

The native Android build schedules local notifications straight from your phone — no relay, no server round-trip, works with the network off.

Optional Telegram

Bring your own bot to log and answer reminders in chat. Off by default, clearly labeled — it crosses the relay in plaintext by design.

Honest about the boundary: the vault is zero-knowledge, but web-delivered crypto trusts the origin, and removing a device retires it rather than rotating keys. Full trust model & caveats are in the docs.

Cloud mode

Runs in your browser

Your AI calls your own provider keys, stored inside the encrypted vault. The operator never sees the prompt or the data. A disclosed trial key is available if you'd rather not bring your own.

Server mode

OAuth-protected MCP

A separate, Pocket-ID-protected endpoint. Point Claude or any MCP client at it, discover operations, and run sandboxed multi-step analyses in a single call.
