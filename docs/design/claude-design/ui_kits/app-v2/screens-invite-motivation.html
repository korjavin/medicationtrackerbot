Why get an invite

Every place that mentions an invite now says what an account adds, in the same four facts. Local mode stays the default and is described as complete. Changes are copy and order only; no new components.

The shared facts

Four, in this order. The privacy fact always sits right after the copy, so "a copy on our server" is never read without "and only you can open it".

  1. Your records on every device · Phone and tablet stay in step. Add one with a code.
  2. A copy off this phone · Lose or reset it, sign back in, and it's all there.
  3. Only you can open it · Encrypted on your device before it syncs. We store it but can't read it.
  4. Open it in any browser · On a laptop or a work computer, whenever you need it.

The motivation is sync only. AI, Telegram and the food database are left out because they work in local mode; the sheet says so in one line. "Sign back in" keeps the copy honest: getting the records back needs the passkey or the Emergency Kit, so the word "backup" is never used for sync.
