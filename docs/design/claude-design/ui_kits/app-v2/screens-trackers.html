Trackers

User-defined count and check trackers live in one card on Today, between the vitals strip and the Goal Line. Tapping a tile logs it. The ⋯ button or a long press opens the log sheet. Create, edit and archive happen in a modal sheet, and there is no new nav section. Beads: med-1vy5.2 (TR1), .3 (TR2), .4 (TR3 strength), .5 (reminders).

Designer decisions (Q5, Q6)

  • Q5 · Weekly and monthly on a daily screen. The tile shows the period total ("2 / 3 this week") and the days left. A weekly tile adds a 7-segment Mon–Sun strip with today ringed. A monthly tile uses a meter instead, because 30 segments don't fit. A tap logs today. Met styling applies when the period is met. Once the period is met, the tile stays green until the period ends.
  • Q6 · Reminder for limits. Hidden, not disabled. A one-line reason replaces it. Switching a goal with a reminder to a limit drops the reminder on Save.

TR1 · Today Trackers card

Inside a T1 Today frame, scrolled to the card. All eight tile states across two times of day, plus the empty state.

TR1b · Tracker log sheet

A custom amount, today's logs with swipe and undo, and links to edit and history.

TR2 · Create / edit tracker

A full-height modal sheet. The template picker comes first on create, and edit opens straight on the form.

TR3 · Tracker detail / history

A pushed page. The current period comes first, then 30 days, then habit strength.
