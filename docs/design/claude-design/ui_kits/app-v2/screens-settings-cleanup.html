Settings cleanup, back and pull to refresh

From Developer's iOS review of 2026-10-25. Settings had two entries that opened the same Devices page, and the switch back to local mode was buried three levels down. The app also ignored the back swipe and had no pull to refresh. Proposed kit addition, in this page's style block: .wg-ptr (the pull indicator).

Answers to the open questions

  • 1 · Where does Devices live? On its own row, Devices & connectors, because that's where anyone looking for devices taps first. The Your vault card stops opening it. The card now opens the Account & privacy page, which keeps its title (owner, 2026-10-25). The Account & privacy row leaves the home list, and its Kit chip moves onto the card. The card's meta drops the device count, since the Devices row already shows it: "address · synced 09:40". The result is one entry per page and one row fewer.
  • 2 · The local-mode switch. It moves to the top level, as the only row in slot ② right under the vault card: "Use this iPhone without an account" with "Keeps a copy here. The account stays on the server." It's shown only in the iOS and Android apps while signed into an account, the same condition as today's Leave section, and it opens the same S9 · leave dialog. The Leave section is removed from the vault page, so the switch lives in one place. It's a plain row, not a sun: it's a mode switch, not the next thing to do. In local mode the Sync & backup card already sits in this spot, so both modes put "change mode" at the top.
  • 3 · Back (no frames). The iOS edge swipe, the Android back gesture and the browser Back all walk one stack, in this order: close the open dialog → close the open sheet → pop the pushed page (a Settings subpage, an editor) → return to the previous tab. On the root of a tab with nothing open, it does nothing on iOS (no Safari, no bounce). On Android it leaves the app, as the system expects. A dialog that's mid-save (D1 with a typed word) closes like Cancel. Unsaved form text gets no extra confirm; it behaves as if Back were tapped in the page bar.
  • 4 · Pull to refresh. Only on Health (every segment) and Today, as Miles asked. On Health it imports from Apple Health / Health Connect, plus a sync pull when there's an account. On Today it does only the sync pull, so in local mode with no health import, Today has no pull. The indicator is a small float pill under the segments, never a full-page spinner: what's on screen stays readable and tappable while it runs (PR2).
  • 5 · What it says when done. Nothing comes new: the pill just goes away, with no "Up to date" toast (owner OK, 2026-10-25). New readings: a top toast "12 new readings from Apple Health" (PR3). A failure (offline, permission revoked): the top toast in the danger style, naming the fix, for example "Health Connect access is off · Settings". No modal.
