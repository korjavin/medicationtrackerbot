Answers to the open questions

  • Why the food sheet and not a new one? Food is the most-logged kind, so Today → Log lands on search. The other kinds are one row of buttons, which saves the in-between sheet's extra tap for food (T3b → F4 was 2 sheets).
  • Where does Also log sit? Under the food paths, above Recent, so it is on screen without scrolling at 390×844. It isn't pinned: the search bar stays the pinned element (med-o7zq.21).
  • Med lists only as-needed meds. Scheduled doses have their slots on Today and the Timeline, and an extra scheduled dose is rare. It goes through My meds → ⋯ → Log a dose, as today.
  • Med's Take logs at now with Undo (the TD rule). Another time = tap the row → existing log_past sheet.
  • Feature flags. Each kind follows its feature switch as the T3b sheet does today. Med needs Meds on and at least one active as-needed med.
  • Rollback. UI only: the Log button's opener and the add sheet's home pane. Med's Take uses the same write as the log_past sheet. No new record field, so reverting brings T3b back.
