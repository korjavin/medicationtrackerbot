Food portions

Logging a food from search, with a sensible starting weight and any amount in one field (Developer go 29443, work topic “food portion 29443”). Supersedes F5’s portion row (100 · 150 · 200 · Other → dialog).

Answers to the open questions

  1. + on search results? Yes (PS1): it’s the 2-tap path to log a known food, and it works the way Recent’s + already does. The row shows the grams the + will log, so nothing is hidden.
  2. Why not 150? Developer asked for 100 · 200 · field. With your last portion in the field, 150 g people get 150 by default.
  3. Show “last time” / “serving” next to the number? No. The number is the label; fewer words on rows (Developer, med-okwf).
  4. Meals start at their own total weight in the field (bolognese 350 g); Recent rows are unchanged.
  5. Typing never logs. Enter/Done closes the pad; only the primary or a + writes.
  6. Scan lands on the same picked row (FC1), so it gets the same portion row and starting weight.
