Food portions

Logging a food from search, with a sensible starting weight and any amount in one field (Developer go 29443, work topic “food portion 29443”). Supersedes F5’s portion row (100 · 150 · 200 · Other → dialog). Chips adapt to each food (Developer 29456).

Answers to the open questions

  1. + on search results: yes (Developer 29456). It logs the selected starting portion that the row shows.
  2. Fixed chips (100 · 150 · 200)? No (Developer 29456): three chips derived per food, plus a field. Order of sources: your history of that food → pack size → typical serving → kcal guess.
  3. Rounding: to 5 g below 50 g, 10 g up to 300 g, 25 g above. Duplicate chips after rounding collapse; the next candidate fills the gap.
  4. Meals (total_weight_g): whole · ½ · your last portion if different, with whole selected. Recent rows are unchanged.
  5. Show “last time” / “serving” words? No, only the “pack” unit on a pack chip. The numbers are the labels (fewer words on rows).
  6. Typing never logs. Only the primary or a + writes. Scan lands on the same picked row (FC1).
