Round 2 (Developer 15503: a US/EU choice in Settings, simpler)
  1. One setting decides the register: "Check names in" US register (default, today's behaviour) or EU register. No editor button, no first-tap opt-in, no on/off switch.
  2. EU chosen: the editor shows an EU chip where the Rx chip is today. The RxNav lookup and the interaction warning don't run, and the choice says so.
  3. Sheet: one card per substance text, route rows with countries. No country chips (dropped for simplicity).
  4. Footer: two short lines; the full attribution text stays in the privacy/about page (Miles .5 to confirm it meets the EMA notice).
