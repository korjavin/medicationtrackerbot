Onboarding v3 · "just open it and use it"

This replaces N1, N2 and the signup placement of O1. The app works on first launch in local mode, sync is a feature in Settings, and invites arrive by link. Everything is built from kit v2.1; the only additions are presentation-only (browser chrome, the desktop frame and the system paste control in kit.css). N3–N6, S6–S9, D1 and A1 are unchanged.

Review of the hypotheses

Weak

  • "Sync is a feature you turn on", but only with an invite. For a store user the Settings card and the W4 nudge would advertise a feature they can't use. The designs say so plainly ("Sync needs an invite for now"). Before launch, decide whether there will be self-serve sign-up or a request-an-invite flow; otherwise backup is the only real protection P1 users have.
  • A time-based nudge (~7 days) is late for meds users, who may enter a full schedule on day one. Trigger on value instead: 20 records or 7 days, whichever comes first.
  • The iPhone hand-off relies on the user returning to Safari. Many will tap "Open" in the App Store and land in local mode with the invite lost. Mitigation: L1 copies a short code when "Get the app" is tapped, and the F1 system paste control picks it up with no prompt.
  • Two screens before Today (picker plus trial), and the trial consent (A1) makes three. Show W2 only when Food is picked, and start the consent in place.
  • Hiding "encryption" entirely costs trust with the privacy-minded users this app attracts. Keep one plain line ("the server can't read it") and the long version in Privacy.

Missing

  • A used or expired invite (single-use, 14 days). This will be the most common failure. Drawn as L1 · expired.
  • An invite into an account that already has records → the D1 merge/replace choice, after sign-in.
  • An invite opened on a phone already syncing to another account → needs a "Switch account?" D1 dialog (not drawn).
  • No install referrer (sideload or a browser hand-off on Android) → the same F1 fallback as iPhone.
  • What "Skip" on W1 means: all modules on, or none. Proposed: all on, which matches today's default.

Answers to the open questions

  • 1 · W1: one screen with check choices. A chat-like flow takes longer to reach the same seven yes/no answers and implies an intelligence that isn't there.
  • 2 · W2: show one real example, sentence in and logged rows out, labelled "Example". Drawing the line at pushy: one screen, a single sun, "Not now" as a full-width secondary, no countdown or scarcity, and never shown again after a skip (the A1 Today card may appear once).
  • 3 · W4: a card on Today, below Next up, never a banner. × snoozes it for 14 days; after the second dismissal only the Settings chip remains. The Sync & backup chip reads "Unprotected" until a backup or sync exists.
  • 4 · F1: on W1, as a small ghost button in the footer, plus on the Sync & backup page. It doesn't go on Today, and it's hidden on Android when a referrer has already signed the user in.
  • 5 · L1: one sentence, saying what the app is, plus the expiry date as a hint. Anything about sync or privacy belongs in the app.
