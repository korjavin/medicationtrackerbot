Copy past entries to Health

Med-8f4s.11. With "Also write my entries" on, the user can copy their past BP (with pulse), weights and completed workouts into Health Connect / Apple Health: offered once after switch-on, and from Settings any time. Readings imported from Health are never written back. Base: HW2 on screens-health-write.html.

Answers to the open questions

Bead med-8f4s.11 (Developer 15566, Miles 15568/15575, Archie 15574). Approved by Developer 15590 with these changes.

  • Offer as a dialog, not a sheet: it's a yes/no with one sentence of counts, the same in-page dialog the app uses for confirms. Copy is the sun.
  • No duplicates in Health (Developer 15590 "it shouldn't show twice, fix it"): before copying, an entry that Health already holds from another app (same kind, same time, same value) is skipped and not counted. No "may show twice" line anywhere. The matching rule is Archie's (.11).
  • One total, no categories (Developer 15590): "304 past entries" in the offer and the result; only a kind that is not allowed is named.
  • Settings action is title only (Developer 15590 yes) ("Copy past entries", no sub-line): Developer's no-descriptions rule. It opens the same dialog with fresh counts.
  • Progress, paused and result all live in that one row of the Health card, with no toast or banner. Paused shows the count so far and says it continues.
  • Apple Health: the same frames, with "Apple Health" in place of "Health Connect".
