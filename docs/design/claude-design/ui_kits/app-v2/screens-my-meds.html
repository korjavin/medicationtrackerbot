My meds

Meds has two sub-tabs: Timeline (what to take and what you took) and My meds (every medication you have, with when you take it and how much is left). The Schedule and Stock sub-tabs merge into My meds. A new prescription is added as a medication, and its times are part of it, so there is no separate "schedule" to find.

MY · My meds

Approved by Developer (owner topic "design: meds stock", 2026-11-12): one My meds sub-tab, name "My meds", one Scheduled group. Replaces M1 and ST4 as the list. The Timeline (TL1–TL4), the Update stock sheet (ST1–ST3) and the editor sections (M5, M6, ST5) stay as they are.

Answers to the open questions

1. Why merge Schedule and Stock? Both are the same list of meds. Schedule rows already carried a stock chip, and Schedule repeated the Timeline's "next dose" buckets. One list named after what it holds ("My meds") answers "where do I add my new prescription?".

2. Where did "Take 3" / "Log late" go? Taking scheduled doses lives on the Timeline (focus card, Take/Skip). My meds no longer groups by the next dose hour. As-needed meds keep Log here, because they have no Timeline slot.

3. Compatibility. Sub-tab ids stay: schedule is My meds, and inventory becomes an alias that opens My meds at "Needs a refill" (like history → timeline today). Stock stays derived from the ledger, and Stop tracking only sets tracking off.

4. Weekly meds. One Scheduled group for daily and weekly meds (Developer: "scheduled looks good"); a weekly row names its days before the times. No separate Weekly group.

5. Archived. Archived meds are hidden from the main list and from every stock warning. The folded row opens them, and un-archiving brings stock back with its history.
