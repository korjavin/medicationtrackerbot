From Developer (2026-11-04): the History list isn't liked, and the next-workout card is hard to read ("Ad hoc" and "Next Day" confuse people). This redraws the Train tab's first sub-tab: one "Up next" card above a week-by-week timeline (WN1–WN6). The session takeover (W1–W4, WA, WS) is unchanged.

Answers to the open questions

My recommendation for each, for Developer to confirm or change.

  • "Ad hoc" → "Start empty workout". It says what you get: a session with no exercises yet, and you add them as you go. On the timeline these sessions carry an "Off plan" tag. Other options: "Free workout", "Quick workout".
  • "Next Day" → "Plan day" choice on the card. All days of the rotation are shown and the current one is selected; a tap picks it. This also covers jumping two days ahead, which Next Day couldn't do in one tap. Code: today's domain only moves to the next day (nextVariant); this needs "set this day" (Archie to scope). Shown only for rotating plans.
  • Statuses become plain words. Upcoming / Today / Ready to Start → the date and time; To Be Skipped → a "Skipping" tag; In Progress → "In progress · 24 min". Snoozed → "Snoozed to 18:30" in the eyebrow.
  • History → Timeline, grouped by week. Each week shows a count, total time and seven day dots, so the last weeks read at a glance. Skipped days are dashed, never red. The sub-tab is renamed; its id stays history.
  • No future list. The card shows only the next plan day. Planned days further ahead stay on the Plans tab.
  • Unchanged: the session takeover, per-set logging, PRs, the gym switch, the Telegram /workout flow, row tap = open session, row swipe = edit/delete.
