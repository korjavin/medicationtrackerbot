Today · doses in one tap

UX challenge #1 + #1b (owner topic “design: UX challenge”, go 25551). A due dose takes in one tap from Today, with Undo. Two or more missed doses fold into one row with Mark all taken / Skip all. Everything else on Today is unchanged from T1.

Answers to the open questions

  • Which Take is one tap? Today's Next up Take for a dose due within the next hour or already due inside the 5-minute grace, the same window the Timeline uses (MEDS_NEXT_TAKE_WINDOW_MS). A dose 1–2 h ahead (Next up looks 2 h ahead) keeps its Take, but the tap opens the Timeline's early sheet, “Due at 15:00. Mark taken now?” (bd med-wt2q: no early take by accident).
  • What time is logged? Now, like the Timeline's row Take. Another time = tap the row → sheet.
  • Undo. A toast, 8 s, on every one-tap Take and on both bulk actions. Undo = POST /api/doses/act {action:'undo'}, which returns the stock (Miles 24966). No dialog.
  • When does the fold start? At 2 missed doses, the Timeline's bulk threshold. Missed = past slot + 5 min grace, not snoozed, any day (today's missed set, missedDosesCell).
  • Mark all taken writes which time? Each dose's planned time, the MT4 rule. That's why Skip all sits beside it (Miles 24966): history stays honest when you really missed them.
  • Sun. Unchanged rule: the first actionable due item. Missed rows and the bulk pair never take it.
  • Rollback. UI only, on the existing doses/act writes. No record field, no migration: reverting the PR restores T1's Next up.
