Meds timeline v2

From Developer's test on every platform (2026-10-25): Tomorrow looked like today, history never ended, and the thing to act on didn't stand out. This revises MT1. Proposed kit additions, in this page's style block: .wg-tl-focus (focus card), .wg-tl-planned (planned days) and .wg-tl-more (the show-more row), .wg-tl-day (a folded planned day).

Answers to the open questions

  • Order (Developer, round 2). Time order: the future is on top and loads more upward with "Show 3 more days ahead"; the past is at the bottom and loads more downward with "Show 3 earlier days". The page opens at its top.
  • Tomorrow vs today. Future days are folded to one planned row each: dashed, dimmed, with a "Planned" tag, no buttons and a count ("3 doses · first 08:20"). A tap unfolds that day's doses, still drawn as planned. Because it's folded, the future takes about one row above Today.
  • History isn't endless. Yesterday and the day before by default, plus 3 days per tap, read locally and instantly. Each visit starts short again.
  • Focus card (Developer). Exactly one pending dose, the latest of any day, with Take as the sun. "+N more pending" is an information count only; the rest are listed under Today's Pending. Then the next dose due today. The empty state names the next dose (TL3).
  • No "missed". Unchanged: a dose stays pending until it's taken or skipped (TL4).
  • Scope. Shared web code over the dose engine and the Phase 2 projection (med-wi09.12). MT2–MT7 keep their rules inside the new blocks.
