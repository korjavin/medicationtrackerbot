Med Tracker Cloud

A self-hosted health tracker built around a zero-knowledge vault. Your vault — every health record you store and sync — is end-to-end encrypted: the keys never leave your devices, and the operator running this service holds only ciphertext it cannot open. You unlock it with a passkey on your own device.

Optional integrations you turn on — Telegram, trial AI, voice, the hosted Claude connector, food lookup — reach outside that vault and are not end-to-end encrypted. Each one is off until you enable it, and each is listed with what it sends and to whom under Settings → What can the operator see?

Invitations only. There is no public signup here — if you were given a personal claim link, open it on the device you want to use, or contact the operator who invited you.
