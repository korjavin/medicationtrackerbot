Your Health Vault, End-to-End Encrypted

How we turned a mess of health apps into one private companion — installable in your browser, with a vault encrypted on your device and unlocked only by you. The optional integrations that reach outside that vault are named in Chapter 7.

The Challenge

Health Data Was Everywhere. And Nowhere.

We started with a simple frustration: keeping track of medications, blood pressure, weight, and sleep required four different apps — each one another company holding your medical data.

None of them talked to each other. None of them gave the full picture. We needed a single source of truth whose vault only you can read.

Step 6: Zero-Knowledge Privacy

Encrypted Before It Leaves Your Device

This is the heart of it. In the cloud edition — the default — every record in your vault is end-to-end encrypted in your browser before it's ever sent. The server stores only that ciphertext; against the vault, a full breach, a subpoena, or a curious operator yields nothing readable.

You unlock with a passkey (Face ID or fingerprint) — there's no password to steal and nothing crackable stored on the server. Devices sync through an encrypted log, and reminders ring via a blind push relay that never learns what they say.

Where the vault ends. A few features are deliberately outside it. Off until you turn them on: Telegram (chat text crosses the relay in the clear both ways), trial AI and trial voice (your prompt, photo, or audio goes through the operator's OpenAI / ElevenLabs account), and the hosted Claude connector (the operator runs the bridge and sees the traffic). With no separate switch, active whenever you use them: the default food database (search terms cross the operator's proxy unless you supply your own endpoint) and drug lookups to RxNav. Bring your own provider key and the AI and food paths stop touching the operator entirely. The app lists all of it under What can the operator see?

No vendor lock. Export everything anytime, or self-host the whole cloud yourself.

  • Zero-Knowledge Vault, End-to-End Encrypted
  • Passkey Unlock — No Passwords
  • Blind Push Relay & Encrypted Sync
  • Integrations Outside the Vault — Each One Disclosed
